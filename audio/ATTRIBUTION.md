# Recorded effects

All recordings below are offered under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

- `steel-*`, `parry-*`: **Ben Jaszczak and Brian Nelson / Still North Media**, [Medieval sound effects — Weapon impacts](https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-sound-effects-weapon-impacts). Extracts of Spear Katana Blade on Blade and Seax Katana Blade on Blade.
- `flesh-*`: **Independent.nu** (submitted by qubodup), [8 wet squish, slurp impacts](https://opengameart.org/content/8-wet-squish-slurp-impacts).
- `impact-*`: **qubodup**, [12 wet towel impacts](https://opengameart.org/content/12-wet-towel-hittingfallingpunching-floor-sounds).
- `snow-*`: **Iwan “qubodup” Gabovitch**, [9 wet snow steps](https://opengameart.org/content/9-wet-snow-steps).
- `swish-*`: **qubodup**, [Swish — bamboo stick weapon swooshes](https://opengameart.org/content/swish-bamboo-stick-weapon-swhoshes).
- `voice-*`: **qubodup**, [15 vocal male strain/hurt/pain/jump sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/15-vocal-male-strainhurtpainjump-sounds).
- `cloth-*`, `scrape-*`: **Jan Schupke / Vehicle**, [Fantasy Weapons and Apparel SFX Library](https://opengameart.org/content/fantasy-weapons-and-apparel-sfx-library). Leather movement and sword scrape Foley.

Game edits: individual impacts extracted, downmixed to mono for spatialization,
resampled with a polyphase antialiasing filter to 48 kHz, quiet leading/trailing
room noise trimmed, peaks normalized to -2 dBFS, short edge fades applied,
exported as PCM 16-bit WAV. Original filenames and excerpt timestamps are in
`sources.json`. These samples are local assets; gameplay does not call a sound service.
