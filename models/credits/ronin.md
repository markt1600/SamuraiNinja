# Samurai Inspired Character

[Samurai Inspired Character](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/samurai-inspired-character-39e823e109044428b274a5c1aacb49bf)
by [Thomas Walker](https://sketchfab.com/ThomasWalker), licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Source: the author's downloadable 4K GLB, supplied by the repository owner.
The downloadable model is static (no skeleton or skin attributes).

ZAN modifications: normalized Y-up coordinates, authored a 53-joint humanoid rig
with four normalized skin influences per vertex, calibrated armor/head rigidity,
kept albedo up to 4K and optimized PBR detail maps to 2K (JPEG color/roughness, lossless PNG normals), and retargeted the rig to the live
combat simulation. The neutral-pose rigging script is `scripts/rig-character.py`.
Skin weights are generated from anatomical landmarks; this is a game-ready
adaptation, not an artist-painted production rig. The original geometry and
texture art remain Thomas Walker's work. No endorsement is implied.
